## Contracting Terms & Primary Policy References

- **Task Order (TO) & PWS:** The specific order and Performance Work Statement defining scope, labor categories, and binding contractor qualification clauses under a contract vehicle.
- **Labor Category (LCAT):** The contractual job classification specifying minimum education, years of experience, security clearance, and any mandatory credentials required for billing against a billet.
- **DCWF Work Role:** The standardized DoD Cyber Workforce Framework role assigned to cyberspace duties. Under DoDM 8140.03, qualification requirements attach to the assigned work role rather than a generic job title.
