Contracting Terms & Primary Policy References

Task Order (TO) & PWS

TERM // 01

The specific order and Performance Work Statement defining scope, labor categories, and binding contractor qualification clauses under a contract vehicle.

Labor Category (LCAT)

TERM // 02

The contractual job classification specifying minimum education, years of experience, security clearance, and any mandatory credentials required for billing against a billet.

DCWF Work Role

TERM // 03

The standardized DoD Cyber Workforce Framework role assigned to cyberspace duties. Under DoDM 8140.03, qualification requirements attach to the assigned work role rather than a generic job title.
